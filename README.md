Steam Dock

Turn your desktop into a console-inspired gaming hub.

Steam Dock is a premium desktop launcher for Windows that gives you instant access to your games, applications, and emulators through a beautiful animated interface inspired by classic console dashboards. Instead of searching through folders or multiple launchers, Steam Dock keeps your favorite titles organized in one clean, customizable dock.

Whether you have a small collection or a library of hundreds of games, Steam Dock provides a fast, elegant way to browse and launch everything with a single click.
